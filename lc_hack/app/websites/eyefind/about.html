About Eyefind

Eyefind reads every website in Los Santos and Blaine County, so you don't have to. Type what you are looking for and we show you where it is: a burger, a bank, a car or a police station.

How we rank

A page with your words in its title comes first, then its address, its description and finally its text. Every word you type has to be on the page.

Add your website

There is nothing to do. If your website is on the internet, Eyefind finds it. Changes show up the next time someone searches.
